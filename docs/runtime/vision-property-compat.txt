Vision property compatibility audit

Failure observed
- Startup passed the projectile and ballistic registry checks from the prior launch.
- Gates of Hell 1.064.0 first failed in `/set/vision/vision.set` while expanding `vision_optic` -> `vision_new_optic_human_cannon` -> `human_in_IR_and_optic_spectre`.
- `eActorVision.cpp:89` rejected `firing_silent_pb` as an incorrect property.
- After the compatibility file collapsed that firing-property family, the next launch advanced to `vision_none` and rejected `cannon_01a` at line 561.
- The second failure proved the root collision was not the individual rules. The later effective `resource/set/vision/settings.set` had replaced Code-X's term registry, leaving both weapon-specific firing terms and modern actor classes undefined.

Source audit
- West-81 `resource/set/vision/vision_generic.inc` SHA-256: `a106438194d8728354cd553a405951aba0950f20a6a122435c6e3c96165eb33d`.
- Code-X `resource/set/vision/vision_generic.inc` SHA-256: `8686a0f57d7b205dd76b1f08d057d1518049c56572fc6c38e0e2f81f6671e600`.
- Code-X `resource/set/vision/settings.set` SHA-256: `0746381873c36b6f1cf29ee5bf247828a927d2b84c384853fcaa1fc623c38310`.
- Code-X is the authoritative modern vision contract because its generic rules and term registry are a matched pair used by the packed modern entities.
- The registry defines `cannon_01a`, vehicle levels 06 through 08, UAV classes, missiles and impactors, and the complete suppressed-weapon, grenade, and knife firing families.
- The retained relative include `new_vision.inc` is bound to the exact Code-X source file SHA-256 `69fa86eeda21addc458e63731f1de8956bdbed44e295e34744254d39b30ef056` in `docs/external-includes.json`.

Compatibility decision
- Own both `resource/set/vision/vision_generic.inc` and `resource/set/vision/settings.set` in mod #5.
- Use the Code-X term registry so every actor and firing property referenced by the modern vision rules is registered before `vision.set` is evaluated.
- Keep the temporary generic-firing collapse for this startup checkpoint. Once startup reaches the menu, restore the exact Code-X weapon-specific visibility blocks under the now-restored registry and verify them in a separate runtime step.
- Do not change weapon files, stealth tags, damage, range, or Dynamic Conquest registration.

Behavioral effect
- Modern actor classes including `cannon_01a`, later vehicle levels, UAVs, missiles, and impactors retain their intended visibility categories.
- Firing remains more visible than idle movement through the generic firing multipliers during this checkpoint.
- The exact suppressed-weapon exceptions remain temporarily collapsed until the startup chain is clear.

Runtime status
- Static contracts are covered by `tests/test_vision_property_compat.py` and `tests/test_vision_settings_compat.py`.
- In-game startup remains unverified until the next manual launch.

Radar helper follow-up
- A subsequent startup passed the restored vision property registry and reached `vision_radar_shipSAM`.
- The compatibility-owned `vision_generic.inc` still referenced `small_UAV_in_rad_spectre` after its three-definition Code-X radar helper block had been omitted.
- Restored the exact Code-X definitions for `stealth_plane_in_rad_spectre_short`, `stealth_plane_in_rad_spectre`, and `small_UAV_in_rad_spectre`.
- Existing radar consumers, ranges, multipliers, and Dynamic Conquest data remain unchanged.
- Focused radar-helper and vision-registry tests passed before the runtime branch was updated.

Whole-contract replacement
- The next startup passed the restored radar helper block and then failed at `("vision_optic" args 70)`.
- The compatibility-edited file declared `vision_optic` as a named `{...}` object while later consumers invoked it as a reusable `(define ...)` macro.
- This proved the remaining vision failures were caused by a structurally mixed `vision_generic.inc`, not isolated missing names.
- Replaced the entire compatibility-edited vision family with the matched Code-X `vision_generic.inc`, `settings.set`, and `new_vision.inc` files.
- Added a static macro-contract test covering all `vision_*` calls in the owned family so undefined vision helpers fail CI before in-game testing.
- No Dynamic Conquest, weapon damage, faction, or Last Victim entity files were changed by this replacement.
