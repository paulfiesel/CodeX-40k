Vision property compatibility audit

Failure sequence
- Startup passed the projectile and ballistic registry checks.
- Gates of Hell 1.064.0 first rejected `firing_silent_pb` while expanding the modern optic rules.
- Restoring the Code-X term registry advanced startup to `cannon_01a` and then to `vision_radar_shipSAM`.
- Restoring the omitted radar-helper block advanced startup to `("vision_optic" args 70)`.
- That final failure proved the compatibility-edited file was structurally mixed: `vision_optic` had been converted into a named `{...}` object while consumers still invoked it as a reusable `(define ...)` macro.

Source audit
- West-81 `resource/set/vision/vision_generic.inc` SHA-256: `a106438194d8728354cd553a405951aba0950f20a6a122435c6e3c96165eb33d`.
- Code-X `resource/set/vision/vision_generic.inc` SHA-256: `8686a0f57d7b205dd76b1f08d057d1518049c56572fc6c38e0e2f81f6671e600`.
- Code-X `resource/set/vision/settings.set` SHA-256: `0746381873c36b6f1cf29ee5bf247828a927d2b84c384853fcaa1fc623c38310`.
- Code-X `resource/set/vision/new_vision.inc` SHA-256: `69fa86eeda21addc458e63731f1de8956bdbed44e295e34744254d39b30ef056`.
- Code-X is authoritative for the modern vision family because these three files form the matched contract used by its packed entities.

Compatibility decision
- Mod #5 owns the exact Code-X `vision_generic.inc`, `settings.set`, and `new_vision.inc` together.
- The temporary generic-firing collapse and manually reconstructed vision objects are removed.
- Reusable contracts including `vision_optic`, `vision_optic_driver`, modern optic generations, radar helpers, and acoustic helpers remain `(define ...)` macros.
- Last Victim remains authoritative for its faction entities and other later-loaded roots; this change is limited to the modern shared vision contract.
- No weapon damage, faction registration, Dynamic Conquest economy, or campaign matchup values are changed.

Validation
- Tests verify all three matched files are locally owned.
- Tests verify `vision_optic` and other reusable contracts remain defines rather than named objects.
- Tests verify every `vision_*` macro call in the owned family resolves to a definition in the owned family.
- Tests verify modern actor terms and radar helpers remain registered.
- Full repository unit tests passed in the one-shot generation workflow before publication to PR #32.

Runtime status
- The prior startup crash at `("vision_optic" args 70)` is addressed by the whole-contract replacement.
- In-game startup remains unverified until the next manual launch.
